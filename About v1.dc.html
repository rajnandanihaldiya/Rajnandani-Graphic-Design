About

I started in graphic design, moved to screens at MCCIA, and now freelance on websites and interfaces — learning to ask why before I draw.

Visual design taught me hierarchy, restraint and how much a single detail can carry. Product design is teaching me the rest: constraints, flows, and the people on the other side of the screen.

On a product team I want to be the designer who makes complicated things feel obvious — and who can show the reasoning behind every screen.
