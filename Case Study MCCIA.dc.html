06 · What I learned

Relevance beats volume

  • 01Post less, say more. The quietest month brought the most growth.
  • 02Speak the audience’s language. Hindi creatives connected with MSMEs where formal English didn’t.
  • 03Show people, not notices. Real startup stories outperformed announcements.
