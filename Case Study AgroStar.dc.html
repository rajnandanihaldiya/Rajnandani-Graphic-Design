AgroStar · Graphic design

Designing for India’s farmers, in their language

Campaigns, product launches, ads and festive greetings for AgroStar, made in Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati for every screen a farmer uses.

Role
Graphic Design Intern
Timeline
Jul – Dec 2024
Languages
Hindi · Marathi · Gujarati
Scope
Campaigns, social, in-app, ads
01 · One idea, every format

The Mega Payment Day campaign, sized for five channels

The same offer had to read in a 400px notification and on a full-screen story. The headline, the “MEGA” mark and the red-gold palette stay fixed; only the layout changes.

02 · Campaign

Red Friday: one look, animated and in three languages

A bold red system built to stand out in a crowded feed, with animated versions for each state.

03 · Product launch

Wheat seeds: real farmers, real fields

A four-post social series. Each variety gets its own farmer and one big number, so the series reads as a set in the feed.

04 · Localisation

Same layout, different script

Devanagari and Gujarati sit differently on the line, so each version is re-balanced by hand rather than swapped as text.

05 · Performance ads

Google ads in square, story and landscape
06 · Festive

Greetings for the festivals farmers celebrate

07 · In-app & engagement

Referrals, loyalty, quizzes and safety tips
